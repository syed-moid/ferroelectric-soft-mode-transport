# Benchmark tables of model A (input tables of v4)

Copied from the benchmark of model A (refits of the ALAMODE example data; penalty scan, reduced-basis refit).
The data lines are unchanged; the first comment line of A1_regeneration.csv, A2_rerun.csv, A3_frequencies.csv and
A3_fits.csv was rewritten to name scripts by their v4 path and runs by their run-map names (no local paths).
In A2_rerun.csv the host suffix of the build ids was dropped (as in the scrub of the v3 manifest).
These tables are not regenerated by `scripts/v4/regenerate_v4.py`; they are inputs of SI Tables S1-S2 and of the A3
rows of the number table.
